    What's this?

    AC brings three on-chain art projects together on one screen.

    Paste a wallet and the page finds its Argonauts and Credits. Pick one of each, choose a layer of the Argonaut, and a Modulo Artifact brings that layer to life. It moves with the Artifact's rules and is painted in the Credit's colors.

    Where it comes from. Everything is read straight from Ethereum: the Argonaut's pixels, the Credit's colors and the Artifact's rules. No database, no images stored somewhere else. Before animating, the page redraws your Argonaut and checks that it matches the one on-chain. If it doesn't, you just see the original.

    Nothing to sign. The page only reads. It never connects to your wallet or asks for a signature, and it works with any wallet, because all of this is public.

    How it connects to Modulo. The motion is Modulo's own engine, the same one behind Modulo Punks. It's rebuilt in your browser from the Artifact's data on-chain, and the page reaches Ethereum through Modulo's connection.

    Unofficial. Argonauts by ack.eth / Muse Facktory · Credits by Jack Butcher · Modulo by ubk.

    Modulo is an art project by ubk that lives entirely on Ethereum. Fully on-chain, self-contained.

    Each Modulo token carries a small program: a grid of cells that changes frame by frame, following simple rules. The rules never change, but the result never stops moving.

    Tokens are placed together in a modulo, and their rules mix. When a modulo is settled, that mix is written to Ethereum for good as an Artifact: a recipe for motion anyone can replay straight from the chain.

    Why it fits with Argonauts and Credits. All three are made of pixels and live fully on-chain, and each one brings what the others are missing:

    • An Argonaut is pixel art built in layers, so any layer can become the canvas.
    • A Credit is its own generative piece, and its colors and pattern become the paint and the starting point.
    • An Artifact is pure motion with no picture of its own. It needs a canvas and paint.
